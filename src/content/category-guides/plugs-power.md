## What renters should look for

**A smart plug is the simplest renter upgrade: it needs only an outlet, changes nothing in the apartment and moves with you.** Plug in a lamp, fan or coffee maker and you can run it on a schedule, switch it by voice or turn it off from your phone. Our roundup of the [best Matter smart plugs for apartments](/blog/best-matter-smart-plugs/) compares compact Wi-Fi and Thread models.

Before buying, look at your outlets. Compact plugs that leave the second outlet free are worth seeking out in apartments with few sockets, and the plug's shape matters behind furniture.

## Key specs and trade-offs

**Matter support and the load rating are the two specs that matter most.**

- **Matter:** a Matter plug works with Apple Home, Google Home and Alexa, so you don't have to rebuy plugs if you switch assistants or move in with someone who uses another one.
- **Wi-Fi or Thread:** Wi-Fi plugs connect straight to your router. Thread plugs need a Thread border router, such as some smart speakers and hubs, and they also extend the Thread mesh for battery sensors and locks.
- **Load rating:** check the maximum load printed on the plug and the device's power draw. High-draw appliances such as space heaters need a plug explicitly rated for them, or a heater with built-in smart controls.
- **Energy monitoring:** some plugs report how much power a device uses, which helps you find what's driving your electric bill.
- **Wi-Fi band:** many plugs connect only on 2.4 GHz.

Smart plugs work best with devices that resume when power returns. A lamp or fan with a physical on switch is ideal; an appliance with a soft-touch button usually stays off after power is restored.

## Landlord and deposit considerations

**Plugs don't alter the property, so they almost never need approval.** The things to avoid are anything that replaces an outlet or wall switch, which touches building wiring. Don't overload old outlets or chain power strips together, and keep plugs away from sinks and damp areas unless they're rated for it.

If your apartment's ceiling lights are on a wall switch, a plug can't help. A small button-pushing robot or smart bulbs are the renter-safe options, and our [smart home for renters guide](/blog/smart-home-for-renters/) shows how plugs fit into a full setup.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Running a window AC or heater on a plug that isn't rated for it.
- Automating devices that don't turn back on after a power cut.
- Blocking the second outlet with a bulky plug.
- Leaving plugs on your account when you move. Reset them and remove the old home from the app.

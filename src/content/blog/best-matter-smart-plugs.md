A **Matter smart plug** is a smart plug certified for Matter, the shared smart home standard from Amazon, Google, Apple and Samsung. Plug a lamp, fan or coffee maker into a Matter smart plug and you can control it from Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home or SmartThings, all at once, with no brand lock-in. For renters, that matters: a Matter smart plug keeps working when you move, change phones or switch voice assistants.

Below are the best Matter smart plugs for apartments, the difference between Wi-Fi and Thread versions, and how to use smart plugs safely in a rental.

## Why renters should buy Matter smart plugs

**Matter smart plugs solve the renter's biggest smart home headache: starting over.** Older smart plugs only worked with the apps and assistants their maker supported. A Matter smart plug pairs with any Matter controller by scanning a setup code, and Matter's multi-admin feature lets roommates on different ecosystems control the same plug.

Matter smart plugs come in two network flavors:

| Matter smart plug type | Connects through | Extra hardware | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Matter over Wi-Fi | Your router | None (plus a Matter controller to set up) | Most renters, lowest cost |
| Matter over Thread | Thread mesh | Thread border router | Apple Home users, big sensor setups |

A Thread border router is built into many devices renters already own, including recent Apple TV and HomePod models, several Amazon Echo speakers and Google Nest hubs.

## Smart plug safety in a rental

Smart plugs are the safest smart home device for renters, but a few rules protect you and your landlord's wiring:

- **Respect the rating.** Check the smart plug's maximum load and never exceed it.
- **Skip heaters and window ACs** unless the plug is rated for that continuous load; choose a [heater with built-in smart controls](/blog/smart-home-for-renters/) instead.
- **Avoid power strips behind furniture.** Smart plugs and adapters can run warm; keep them ventilated.
- **Use outdoor-rated plugs outside.** Balconies and patios need weather-resistant smart plugs.

## Best ways to use a Matter smart plug in an apartment

1. **Lamp scheduling.** Turn living room lamps on at sunset, a simple "someone's home" signal while you travel.
2. **Coffee on a timer.** Works with simple switch-style coffee makers that brew when power returns.
3. **Standby power killer.** Cut power to the TV, console and soundbar overnight.
4. **Fan automation.** Pair a fan with a temperature sensor to cool a bedroom only when needed.
5. **Holiday lights.** Schedule decorations without crawling behind the tree.

Smart plugs pair naturally with the other renter-safe upgrades in our [smart home for renters guide](/blog/smart-home-for-renters/), and with a [retrofit smart lock](/blog/best-smart-lock-for-renters/) you can build "arrive home" automations that unlock the door and turn on the lamps together.

## How to set up a Matter smart plug

1. Install your platform's app (Alexa, Google Home, Apple Home or SmartThings) and make sure you have a Matter controller, such as a compatible smart speaker, display or Apple TV/HomePod.
2. Plug in the Matter smart plug near your router for setup.
3. Scan the Matter QR code on the plug or box. **Photograph the code** before you lose the box; you'll need it after a move.
4. Name the plug by room and device ("Bedroom Lamp") so voice commands make sense.
5. To share with another ecosystem, use the "share with another app" or "multi-admin" option in the first app.

## How to install LED strips without damaging paint

**Never stick LED strip lights straight onto painted walls in a rental; put them on furniture, clips or a layer of painter's tape instead.** Our renter method:

1. **Pick a non-wall surface:** under cabinets, behind the TV, under the bed frame or the back of a shelf.
2. **If it must go on a wall, lay painter's tape first** and stick the strip to the tape.
3. **Or use mounting clips** held by removable adhesive strips.
4. **Clean the surface** with rubbing alcohol and let it dry.
5. **Plan the run before you peel** the backing; cut sections usually can't be rejoined.

## Best places for LED strips in a rental

**The best spots for LED strips in an apartment are the ones where the adhesive never touches wall paint.**

- **Behind the TV** for soft backlighting that's easier on the eyes.
- **Under kitchen cabinets** for task light on the counter.
- **Under the bed frame** for a low night light.
- **Along shelves and desks** for a clean accent.
- **Behind a headboard** for a reading glow.

Pair a strip with a [Matter smart plug](/blog/best-matter-smart-plugs/) or your voice assistant so it turns on with the rest of your lights.

## How to remove LED strips at move-out

**Warm the adhesive and peel slowly at a low angle; pulling fast is what tears paint.**

- Run a hair dryer over a short section for 20 to 30 seconds.
- Peel the strip back along the surface, not straight out.
- Slide dental floss behind stubborn spots and saw gently.
- Clean leftover residue with rubbing alcohol on a soft cloth.
- Touch up any small paint lift with the matching paint if your landlord provides it.

## What to look for in LED strip lights

**The best LED strip lights for renters balance light quality, app features and an adhesive backing that you can work around.** These are the details we compare:

- **Light type:** standard strips show small dots of light; a COB LED strip gives a continuous line, which looks better under cabinet edges and on open shelves.
- **Color control:** RGBIC strips show several colors at once, while basic RGB strips show one color along the whole length.
- **Smart home support:** check for Alexa and Google Home support if you want voice control, or the Hue app if you're building around Philips Hue.
- **Length and cutting:** measure first; most strips can be cut at marked points but not rejoined without connectors.
- **Adhesive backing:** strong adhesive is good on furniture but risky on painted walls, so plan the mounting surface before you buy.

## Bedroom and living room ideas

**In a bedroom, a strip under the bed frame or behind the headboard gives a soft night light without any wall contact.** In the living room, a strip behind the TV adds bias lighting that makes the screen easier on the eyes. Set a warm white scene for evenings and save the color effects for movie nights or gaming. Each of these spots keeps the strip off painted walls, which is exactly what protects your deposit.

## Do LED strips need a hub?

**Govee strips connect straight to 2.4GHz Wi-Fi, while Philips Hue strips work over Bluetooth and add features with the Hue Bridge.** If you have only one or two strips, Wi-Fi is simplest; if you're building a larger Hue system, the Bridge keeps things stable.
